SIGN CODE CHEAT SHEET

Madison stops (Wall Triana · County Line · Town Madison · most of Hwy 72 W) → use MADISON numbers only. Never quote Huntsville EMC sizes in Madison.

MADISON (Art. 9 · July 2026)

  • Monument preferred. Pole signs only ≤1,000 ft of I-565.
  • B-1: 20–40 sf / 6 ft  ·  B-2: 40–100 sf / 10–12 ft
  • B-3 / UC / M: 55–155 sf / 15 ft
  • EMC / Digital: ONLY B-2 or B-3 · lot ≥1 acre · major-road frontage
  • 350 ft setback from any residential lot line
  • Static messages only · min 10-sec dwell · auto-dim (≤0.3 fc over ambient)
  • One EMC per lot · must black-out on malfunction

HUNTSVILLE (Art. 72)

  • EMC allowed on ground/monument/pylon in most commercial districts
  • Prohibited in Research Park, RP Applications, RP West, Planned Industrial
  • Electronic face ≤ 75% of total sign area
  • Heights typically 10–35 ft by district · auto-dim required · no animation

Field rule

  • Confirm city limits before quoting size or digital capability.
  • When in doubt: Madison permit portal or Huntsville GIS.
